### 🛰️ What we're building

MODIS is reaching the end of its mission. VIIRS sees fire at a different resolution and sensitivity. We are building a bridge between both sensors so that **25 years of active-fire history stay consistent and usable**, delivered as an open, explorable burning-activity calendar.
